### 📦 1. [Olist Commercial Operations, Delivery SLA & Retention Decision Suite](https://github.com/Daizy-Mgmt/olist-delivery-satisfaction-analytics) *(Flagship BI & BA Suite)*
> **PostgreSQL 15 Star Schema · 14 Power BI DAX Measures · Multi-Lever SLA & Breakeven Simulator · Formal BA/TPM BRD & RACI**

[![Executive Dashboard Preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Daizy-Mgmt/olist-delivery-satisfaction-analytics/main/docs/images/executive_dashboard_preview.png)](https://daisy-olist-analytics.streamlit.app)
[![Multi-Lever SLA Breakeven Simulator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Daizy-Mgmt/olist-delivery-satisfaction-analytics/main/docs/images/sla_simulator_preview.png)](https://daisy-olist-analytics.streamlit.app)

* **The Business Problem:** Modeled **99,441 Brazilian marketplace orders ($13,591,643.70 GMV)** across 2016–2018 into an executive SLA decision engine to diagnose post-purchase review collapse and seller fulfillment bottlenecks.
* **The 4 Headline Discoveries:**
  1. **The 8-Day Satisfaction Cliff:** On-time deliveries maintain **4.29 ★** (`9.3%` negative). Delays exceeding 8 days trigger **79.3% negative (1–2 star) reviews** with average rating crashing to **1.70 / 5.0 ★**.
  2. **Extreme Bottleneck Concentration (Pareto 80/20):** **10% of active sellers (68 of 682 merchants) drive 52.5% of all late delivery items** (`$438.9K` GMV pool). Enforcing SLA gates on Quintile 5 sellers (`19.4%` avg late rate) resolves over half of all delivery breaches.
  3. **Cohort Base-Rate Nuance:** While 1–7 day first-order delays reduce repeat buying by **22.6% relative (`3.40% ➔ 2.63%`)**, Olist's overall repeat baseline is structurally **3.12% (96.88% one-time buyers)**—meaning the primary commercial cost of 8+ day delays is the **79.3% 1–2★ review spike** poisoning conversion and CS ticket volume.
  4. **Regional Corridor Disparities:** Remote Northern/Northeastern states (`MA`, `CE`, `BA`, `PA`) average 19.3–23.7 transit days with Maranhão (`MA`) falling to **82.57% on-time fulfillment**, vs. **95.51%** (`8.7` days) in São Paulo.
* **Deliverables:**
